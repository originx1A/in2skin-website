Microneedling / NANO Needling

Home · Treatments · Microneedling / NANO Needling

Ultra-fine nano tips help serums absorb deeply — in just 20 minutes. Microneedling and NANO needling are the same treatment — only the needle size differs, and the studio picks the size that's best for your face at the visit.

What’s included

  • Gentle cleanse and skin preparation
  • Nano infusion of a professional serum chosen for your skin
  • Soothing finish to seal in the benefits
  • Pairs beautifully with either facial

Benefits

  • Serums absorb deeply for visible results
  • Skin looks plumper, smoother and more radiant
  • Fine lines appear softer and skin tone looks more even
  • Just twenty minutes, added to any facial

Questions

How does it feel?+

Light & gentle — a soft gliding sensation while you relax.

What does it do for my skin?+

Serums reach where they work best — plumper, smoother, more radiant skin.

Can I carry on with my day afterwards?+

Yes — leave glowing and head straight back to your day.

How soon will I see a difference?+

Glow right away, improving over days. A series builds best results.
