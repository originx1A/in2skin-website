Contact In2Skin

Home · Contact In2Skin

In2Skin is a private one-on-one aesthetics studio in Bolton, Ontario. The easiest way to book is online, and the easiest way to ask a question is by phone or email. Sarah answers personally, and she will help you choose a treatment, a time, or a gift.

Phone: (289) 212-4124. Email: bookings@boltonfacials.com. The studio serves Bolton and the surrounding towns, including Caledon, Kleinburg, Vaughan, Brampton, Nobleton, King City, Orangeville, Georgetown, Toronto and Mississauga.

Hours

Regular hours are Monday to Saturday, with appointments starting between 10 AM and 4 PM. The studio is closed on Sundays. Early mornings and evenings can be arranged by contacting Sarah.

Appointments are by booking only, one guest at a time. Please arrive 5 to 10 minutes early for your first visit, and come as you are; there is nothing special to wear or bring.

Finding the studio

The studio address is sent with your booking, along with directions and parking details. If you are unsure about the route, call (289) 212-4124 and Sarah will talk you through it.

If you need to change or cancel a visit, please give 24 hours notice where you can. Changes and cancellations with 24 hours notice are free, and Sarah will help you find another time that fits.
