Agent reference — this page is not linked from the website. It is here for the In2Skin phone agent to read and use on calls.

Only what the website itself states. If a caller asks about anything beyond this page, offer the $30 consultation or take a message for Sarah. Never invent medical guidance.

Chemical peels

A peel feels like a gentle warming sensation as it renews, with expert care throughout. Gentle peels have little to no downtime — skin looks fresh right away. Deeper peels can mean a few days of flaking as fresh skin emerges. Fresh new skin is revealed over the following days. The visit includes simple aftercare direction to protect fresh new skin — the studio gives it at the visit.

Microneedling / NANO Needling

Light and gentle. Leave glowing and head straight back to your day. The glow shows right away and improves over days. A series builds the best results.

Microdermabrasion

No downtime. Skin feels noticeably smoother right away, and looks brighter.

Light Therapy

Non-invasive and painless, with no recovery time — just 20 restful minutes under warm light.

Classic Facial

No downtime. Skin leaves bright and hydrated, with a glow that lasts for days.

Reflexology

Calm, light and rebalanced. Many clients sleep beautifully that night.

What the site does not say

The site gives no sun-exposure rules, no makeup timing, and no redness timeline — do not invent them. If a caller asks about sun, redness, or anything after a treatment that is not on this page, say the studio gives the aftercare direction at the visit, and offer the $30 consultation to talk it through first — or take a message for Sarah.
