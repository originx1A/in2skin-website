Agent reference — this page is not linked from the website. It is here for the In2Skin phone agent to read and use on calls.

The studio’s rules, in plain words, for the phone agent. If a caller asks about anything not on this page, offer the $30 consultation or take a message for the studio.

Booking & deposit

A $30 deposit holds the time when the caller books. The hold lasts 30 minutes — if the caller does not confirm and pay within 30 minutes, the time opens back up and they need to book again.

Book online or by phone. Bookings run tomorrow through 60 days ahead. At most 4 clients a day.

How to pay

Pay in full now, or $30 now and the rest at the visit. The balance is paid at the visit by cash or e-transfer in person, or by card through a link on the client’s own phone. Card charges show as IN2SKIN on the statement. No e-transfer ahead of time, and there is no published e-transfer email.

Hours

Monday to Saturday, appointments start between 9:00 AM and 4:00 PM. Sundays closed. Each visit has 60 minutes of reset time after it before the next client.

Reschedule & no-show

Reschedule is free with 24 hours’ notice. If a client no-shows or cancels late, the deposit is never refunded in cash and never lost — it carries as credit to their next appointment.

Changing a booking

To move a booking, the caller calls the studio at (289) 212-4124 and the studio moves it for them.

Private Hours

Early mornings and evenings, by request — the studio opens early or stays late, just for one client. Private Hours are the regular service price plus $15 once per visit. Times are not published. Sundays closed. The $30 consultation is $30 flat — no Private Hours premium, even after hours.

Gift certificates & vouchers

Full detail is on the gifts page. In short: a gift code looks like IN2-XXXX. Any leftover stays on the code for the person who used it. A voucher dies when the treatment is marked completed and its balance is $0. The dollar value never expires. No cash value. A no-show leaves the voucher valid.

Referrals

Every 3 paid referrals earns $15 of treatment credit plus a free Reflexology add-on. A referred friend counts only on a booking worth at least $85 — a full facial. The credit is treatment credit only, never cash.

First visit

A consultation first, then the treatment — with every step explained as it happens. Full detail is on the first-visit page.

What the agent never shares

Never tell a caller where the studio is or how to get there — that information is shared only when they book. Never read out Sarah’s phone number. Never mention anything private about the business. The studio is in Bolton, Ontario — that is all a caller needs to hear before booking.
