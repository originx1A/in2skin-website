Microdermabrasion

Home · Treatments · Microdermabrasion

A polishing wand sweeps away dull surface skin in 20 minutes — refined texture, instant radiant glow, with no downtime. Great before a facial.

What’s included

  • Gentle cleanse and skin preparation
  • Precision polishing of the skin's surface
  • Soothing, hydrating finish
  • A beautiful base for serums and makeup alike

Benefits

  • Skin feels noticeably smoother right away
  • A brighter, more even-looking complexion
  • Makeup sits more beautifully on polished skin
  • Helps your skincare products work even better

Questions

How does it feel?+

A gentle polishing massage, finished with soothing hydration.

What will my skin look like afterwards?+

Smoother, brighter, fresh — a favourite before events & photos.

Is it suited to my skin?+

Adjusted to your skin that day. Lovely for most skin types.

About every 4 weeks. A series builds an even, refined finish.
