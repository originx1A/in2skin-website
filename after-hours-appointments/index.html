After-hours appointments

Home · Occasions · After-hours appointments

Not everyone can make a daytime appointment. Work, school runs and family commitments fill the middle of the day, and a facial treatment should not have to wait for a free afternoon that never comes. In2Skin offers early-morning and evening visits, arranged by contacting Sarah, for anyone whose days are spoken for.

These visits are the same private, one-on-one appointments as the daytime ones: one guest, one calm room, and a treatment chosen for your skin that day.

How after-hours visits work

Regular hours are Monday to Saturday, with appointments starting between 10 AM and 4 PM, and the studio is closed on Sundays. Early mornings and evenings sit outside those hours and are arranged by contacting Sarah, who will find a time that fits around your day.

To arrange one, call (289) 212-4124 or write to bookings@boltonfacials.com and say what kind of time you need. The booking is confirmed in the same way as a daytime visit, and directions are sent with your booking.

A longer visit, when the day is done

Evening visits suit the longer treatments, because there is nothing after them to rush back to. The Anti-Aging Facial, 80 minutes, is a slow treatment with two masks and an arm massage while the second mask is on, and The Works pairs a facial treatment with red light and reflexology at the same time.

If you would rather keep it short, the Classic Facial, 55 minutes, is a complete treatment on its own, and a 20-minute consultation beforehand can be done by video if that is easier than another trip.

Questions

How do I book an early-morning or evening appointment?+

Contact Sarah by calling (289) 212-4124 or writing to bookings@boltonfacials.com, and say what kind of time you need. Early mornings and evenings are arranged with her directly.

Are after-hours visits the same as daytime ones?+

Yes. The same private, one-on-one appointment, the same treatments, and the same care, at a time that fits your day.

Is the studio open on Sundays?+

No. The studio is closed on Sundays. Regular hours are Monday to Saturday, with appointments starting between 10 AM and 4 PM.
