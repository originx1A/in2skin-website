How to choose

Home · How to choose

Choosing a facial treatment should not feel like a test. There are two main facial treatments at In2Skin, a handful of shorter treatments that can be added to them or booked on their own, and one longer visit that brings several things together at once. This page sets them out in plain words, so you can choose with confidence.

If you would still rather talk it through, the 20-minute consultation, video or in person, is made for first-time clients, and Sarah will recommend a treatment based on your skin, not on a menu.

Classic Facial, 55 minutes

The Classic Facial is the everyday facial treatment, and the best first visit. It covers a deep cleanse, steam, extractions if needed, a hydration mask, and a facial massage that includes the neck and décolletage, with Circadia professional skincare matched to your skin that day.

Choose it if you want clean, settled, hydrated skin, if you have never had a facial treatment, or if you are booking a regular visit every four to six weeks. It suits most skin, including sensitive and acne-prone skin, and it has no downtime.

Anti-Aging Facial, 80 minutes

The Anti-Aging Facial includes all of the Classic Facial, then adds a second mask, an arm massage while that second mask is on, and serums with Circadia products worked into the masks and matched to your skin.

Choose it if your skin feels dry or tired, if fine lines are your main concern, or if you simply want a longer, slower visit. It is the facial treatment to book when the treatment itself is the point of the trip, rather than something fitted between errands.

Shorter treatments and The Works

Microneedling and NANO Needling are one treatment, 20 minutes, with Sarah choosing the needle size at the visit. Microdermabrasion, Light Therapy and Reflexology are each 20 minutes. Any of them can be added to a facial treatment or booked on its own, and Sarah will say plainly whether one suits your skin.

Chemical Peels, 55 minutes including a consultation, are matched to your skin before anything is decided, with the strength chosen at the consultation. The Works is a facial treatment with red light and reflexology running at the same time, so one visit covers skin, rest and tired feet together.

Questions

I have never had a facial treatment. Where do I start?+

The Classic Facial, 55 minutes, is the best first visit. If you would like guidance first, book the 20-minute consultation, video or in person.

What is the difference between the Classic and Anti-Aging facial treatments?+

The Anti-Aging Facial includes everything in the Classic Facial, then adds a second mask, an arm massage while that mask is on, and serums with Circadia products matched to your skin. It is 80 minutes, and it suits dry, tired skin and fine lines.

Can I add a shorter treatment to a facial treatment?+

Yes. Microneedling and NANO Needling, Microdermabrasion, Light Therapy and Reflexology can each be added to a facial treatment or booked on their own. Sarah will advise what suits your skin.
