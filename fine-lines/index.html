Facials for Fine Lines

Home · Skin Concerns · Facials for Fine Lines

Fine lines soften when skin is firm, hydrated and making fresh collagen. At In2Skin, treatments for fine lines combine firming massage, targeted anti-aging serums and rich hydration — and for deeper work, collagen induction that prompts the skin's own repair.

Results build with regular visits. Skin looks firmer and smoother right away, and keeps improving over a series of treatments.

How treatments help fine lines

An anti-aging facial works on the surface and underneath: deep cleansing and exfoliation, firming and lifting massage, targeted serums, and rich hydration.

NANO Needling goes deeper — ultra-fine nano tips open shallow channels on the skin's surface so professional serums absorb more deeply, for plumper, smoother, more radiant skin.

Treatments that help

  • Anti-Aging Facial — $125 · 80 minutes. Firming massage, targeted anti-aging serums and rich hydration, for firm, youthful skin.
  • NANO Needling — $65 · 20 minutes. Ultra-fine nano tips help serums absorb more deeply, for plumper, smoother, more radiant skin.

Good to know

NANO Needling is $65 for 20 minutes. Private Hours, early mornings and evenings by request, are the regular price plus $15 per visit.

Questions

What is the best facial for fine lines?+

The Anti-Aging Facial, $125 for 80 minutes — firming massage, targeted serums and rich hydration, for firmer, smoother, brighter skin.

What is NANO Needling?+

Ultra-fine nano tips open shallow channels on the skin's surface so professional serums absorb more deeply. Skin looks plumper, smoother and more radiant, and fine lines appear softer. It's $65 for 20 minutes.

How often should I book?+

Every 4 to 6 weeks. Results build with regular visits.

Can I add anything to a facial for fine lines?+

Yes — NANO Needling, $65, helps serums absorb more deeply, and Light Therapy, $55, supports the skin's natural renewal.
