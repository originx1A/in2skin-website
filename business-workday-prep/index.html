Facials before the workday

Home · Occasions · Facials before the workday

Some weeks ask more of your face than others: a presentation, a run of meetings, a photograph, or a day when you simply need to look rested. A facial before that kind of week is not vanity; it is preparation, the same as pressing a suit or preparing notes.

In2Skin is a private studio in Bolton, one guest at a time. Regular appointments start between 10 AM and 4 PM, Monday to Saturday, and early mornings and evenings can be arranged by contacting Sarah for anyone whose working day does not leave room in the middle of it.

Look rested, without downtime

The Classic Facial, 55 minutes, is the straightforward choice before a busy week. A deep cleanse, steam, extractions if needed, a hydration mask, and a facial massage including the neck and décolletage leave skin clean and settled, with no downtime, so you can go straight back to your day.

Light Therapy, 20 minutes, can be added to the same visit for a calmer, more even look. It pairs naturally with a facial and needs no recovery time, which is why professionals often add it before a week of being seen.

Making it a habit, not a one-off

A facial every four to six weeks keeps skin steady through long weeks, late nights and dry office air, rather than needing a rescue the week something important happens. The routine at home is kept simple, with Circadia professional skincare matched to your skin, so the results of a visit last until the next one.

If you are unsure what your skin needs, the 20-minute consultation, video or in person, is open to first-time clients, and it can be done by video if you would rather not travel for a conversation.
