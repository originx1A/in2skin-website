Privacy — how In2Skin handles your information

Plain words, no legal maze. Last updated November 18, 2026.

What we collect when you book

Your name and phone number — we need those to hold your appointment and reach you if anything changes. Your email address is optional; if you give it, your confirmation and reminder go there. We also keep the booking itself — the treatment, the day and time, what you paid and how — and any gift certificate or referral code you used. If you buy a gift for a friend, we keep their name and email so the gift can reach them.

Payments — we never see your card

Card payments go through Stripe, on Stripe's own secure checkout page. Your card number never touches our website or our computers — we only ever see the amount, the last four digits on your receipt, and that the payment went through. Cash and e-transfer payments are recorded the same way, without card details of any kind.

Why we need it

Only to run the studio: to hold your time, confirm and remind you, take payment, keep your gift balance straight, and give you referral credit you earned. Nothing else.

Who sees it

Only two people at the studio see it: the esthetician who treats you, and the studio owner. Your information is never sold, rented, or shared with anyone else. Two services touch it only to do their job — Stripe (to take card payments) and Resend (to send your confirmation emails) — and neither of them sells it either.

How long we keep it

Booking and payment records are kept as the studio's business records. Gift certificate balances are kept until they are used up, because a code never expires. If you would like your details removed, email us and we will delete what the law lets us delete — payment records the tax rules require us to keep stay, everything else goes.

Visiting the website

We use Google Analytics to see, in aggregate, how many people visit and which pages they read — no names, no phone numbers. Your browser's address, and the fact that you visited, are visible to our hosting provider (Vercel) in the same way every website's host sees its visitors. We do not track you across other websites.

Questions, or want your details removed?

Email hello.funderwood@gmail.com or call (289) 212-4124 and we will sort it out.
