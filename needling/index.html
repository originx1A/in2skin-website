Microneedling / NANO Needling

Home · Treatments · Microneedling / NANO Needling

Microneedling and NANO needling are one treatment — the same thing, just the needle size. Fine needles create controlled micro-channels that prompt your skin's natural repair and new collagen, while ultra-fine nano tips help serums absorb deeply. Sarah picks the size that's best for your face at the visit.

Questions

What does needling do?+

It creates controlled micro-channels that prompt new collagen — improving the look of fine lines, acne scarring, texture and pores over a series. The nano size infuses serums deeply for plumper, smoother skin.

How does it feel?+

Light & gentle — a soft gliding sensation while you relax.
