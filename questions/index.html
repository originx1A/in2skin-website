Questions

How much does a facial cost in Bolton?+

At In2Skin, the Classic Facial is $85 for 55 minutes and the Anti-Aging Facial is $125 for 80 minutes. Add-ons run $35 to $65, chemical peels run $125 to $475, and Microneedling / NANO Needling is $65.

What does a facial do?+

A facial deep-cleanses, exfoliates, steams, massages and hydrates your skin, finished with a mask matched to your skin that day. Skin leaves brighter, fresher and more hydrated — with a glow that lasts for days.

How long does a facial take?+

The Classic Facial takes 55 minutes and the Anti-Aging Facial takes 80 minutes. Add-ons take 20 minutes each, and can be added to a facial or booked on their own.

How often should I get a facial?+

Every 4 to 6 weeks. Most clients make it a monthly ritual.

What is a first visit like?+

A consultation first, then the treatment — with every step explained as it happens. It is tailored to your skin that day.

What are Private Hours?+

Our premier experience: the studio opens early or stays late, just for you. Start your day glowing before a big meeting, or unwind in the evening after a full day. Your time, your schedule, your treatment. Private Hours are your regular service plus $15.

Can I buy a gift certificate?+

Yes — gift a Classic Facial ($85), an Anti-Aging Facial ($125), The Works ($160), or any amount you choose. A certificate code is emailed to the recipient, ready to redeem when they book.

How do I book?+

Book online anytime on this website, or call (289) 212-4124. You hold your time with a $30 deposit by card when you book online — the balance is paid at your visit — and receive an email confirmation.

Where is the studio?+

In2Skin is a private, one-on-one aesthetics studio in Bolton, Ontario. Appointments are by booking, and directions are shared when you book.

Can I book an appointment for a friend?+

Yes — in the online booking wizard, choose 'For a Friend' and add your friend's name, phone and email. The confirmation goes straight to them, and they just show up.

What payment methods do you take?+

Card online at booking (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay), or cash or Interac e-transfer at the studio. Card charges show as IN2SKIN on the statement.

What if I need to change my booking?+

Call (289) 212-4124 and the studio will move it for you.

What are the benefits of a facial?+

A facial deep-cleanses, exfoliates and hydrates your skin, boosts circulation and leaves skin brighter, softer and glowing. Regular facials keep pores clear, help breakouts settle and slow the look of ageing — and the hour itself is deeply relaxing.

What should I expect during a facial?+

A consultation first, then a deep cleanse, steam, extractions if needed, gentle exfoliation, a relaxing face, neck and décolletage massage, a hydration mask matched to your skin, and serum and moisturizer to finish.

Why are your facials less expensive than other Bolton spas?+

In2Skin is a small private studio — one room, one guest at a time — so a Classic Facial is $85 for 55 minutes. For comparison, a medi-spa in Bolton lists its 60-minute facials at $120.

Is red light therapy safe?+

Yes — red light therapy is non-invasive and painless, with no recovery time. You lie back under warm red light for 20 restful minutes.

What is reflexology?+

Reflexology is gentle, rhythmic pressure on points on your feet that map to the rest of your body — the arch to your back, the toes to your head and neck.

How much downtime is there after a chemical peel?+

It depends on the strength of the peel. Gentle peels have little to no downtime — skin looks fresh right away.

What is the best facial in Bolton?+

There is no single best facial — the best first facial at In2Skin is the Classic Facial, $85 for 55 minutes, tailored to your skin that day. If fine lines are your main concern, the Anti-Aging Facial, $125 for 80 minutes, adds firming massage and targeted serums.

Where can I get Microneedling / NANO Needling near Bolton?+

At In2Skin, a private, one-on-one aesthetics studio in Bolton, Ontario — call (289) 212-4124 to book. Microneedling / NANO Needling uses ultra-fine nano tips to help professional serums absorb more deeply, for plumper, smoother, more radiant skin.

What is the difference between a facial and a chemical peel?+

A facial deep-cleanses, exfoliates, steams, massages and hydrates your skin, finished with a hydration mask — the Classic Facial is $85 for 55 minutes.

Do I need to book a facial in advance in Bolton?+

Yes — advance booking is recommended at In2Skin to get the time you want. You can book online anytime on the website or call (289) 212-4124, and you hold your time with a $30 deposit by card when you book online, the balance paid at your visit.

How much does The Works facial cost in Bolton?+

The Works is $160 with a Classic Facial (55 minutes) or $195 with an Anti-Aging Facial (80 minutes) at In2Skin in Bolton. It is a facial, red light therapy and reflexology all at the same time, while the mask works.

Can I book a facial early in the morning or on a Sunday in Bolton?+

Early mornings and evenings are available as Private Hours, by request — call (289) 212-4124 or email bookings@boltonfacials.com and the studio will set it up. Sundays the studio is closed.

Is there a facial for teenagers with acne in Bolton?+

Yes — the Classic Facial, $85 for 55 minutes, is the recommended affordable first step for teen acne at In2Skin in Bolton. It focuses on a thorough deep cleanse, gentle exfoliation and calming, hydrating care, with every step explained as it happens.
