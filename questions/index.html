Questions

How much does a facial cost in Bolton?+

At In2Skin, the Classic Facial is $85 for 55 minutes and the Anti-Aging Facial is $125 for 80 minutes. Add-ons run $35 to $65, chemical peels run $125 to $475, and microneedling is $65. First visits get $10 off any service.

What does a facial do?+

A facial deep-cleanses, exfoliates, steams, massages and hydrates your skin, finished with a mask matched to your skin that day. Skin leaves brighter, fresher and more hydrated — with a glow that lasts for days.

How long does a facial take?+

The Classic Facial takes 55 minutes and the Anti-Aging Facial takes 80 minutes. Add-ons take 20 minutes each, and can be added to a facial or booked on their own.

How often should I get a facial?+

Every 4 to 6 weeks. Most clients make it a monthly ritual.

What is a first visit like?+

A consultation first, then the treatment — with every step explained as it happens. It is tailored to your skin that day, and your first visit is $10 off any service.

What are Private Hours?+

Premier appointments reserved just for you — early mornings and evenings, by request. You pay the regular price of the services you pick, plus $15 per visit. Sundays the studio is closed.

Can I buy a gift certificate?+

Yes — gift a Classic Facial ($85), an Anti-Aging Facial ($125), The Works ($160), or any custom amount from $10. A certificate code is emailed to the recipient, ready to redeem when they book, and gift codes never expire. You can also buy a treatment voucher: a named facial, paid ahead.

How do I book?+

Book online anytime on this website, or call (289) 212-4124. You pay in full securely by card when you book online and receive an email confirmation. Cash or Interac e-transfer are also welcome at the studio. Advance booking is recommended to get the time you want.

Where is the studio?+

In2Skin is a private, one-on-one aesthetics studio in Bolton, Ontario. Appointments are by booking, and directions are shared when you book. Clients visit from Caledon, Kleinburg, Vaughan, Brampton, Nobleton, King City, Orangeville, Georgetown, Mississauga and across Toronto and the GTA.

What are the benefits of a facial?+

A facial deep-cleanses, exfoliates and hydrates your skin, boosts circulation and leaves skin brighter, softer and glowing. Regular facials keep pores clear, help breakouts settle and slow the look of ageing — and the hour itself is deeply relaxing.

What should I expect during a facial?+

A consultation first, then a deep cleanse, steam, extractions if needed, gentle exfoliation, a relaxing face, neck and décolletage massage, a hydration mask matched to your skin, and serum and moisturizer to finish. Every step is explained as it happens, and you can ask for anything to be gentler or skipped.

Why are your facials less expensive than other Bolton spas?+

In2Skin is a small private studio — one room, one guest at a time — so a Classic Facial is $85 for 55 minutes. For comparison, a medi-spa in Bolton lists its 60-minute facials at $120.

Is red light therapy safe?+

Yes — red light therapy is non-invasive and painless, with no recovery time. You lie back under warm red light for 20 restful minutes. It supports collagen and a calmer, more even complexion.

What is reflexology?+

Reflexology is gentle, rhythmic pressure on points on your feet that map to the rest of your body — the arch to your back, the toes to your head and neck. It helps the whole body unwind, and many clients sleep beautifully that night. It is $35 for 20 minutes, on its own or added to a facial.

How much downtime is there after a chemical peel?+

It depends on the strength of the peel. Gentle peels have little to no downtime — skin looks fresh right away. Deeper peels can mean a few days of flaking or peeling as fresh skin emerges. Your consultation matches the peel to your skin and confirms exactly what to expect, before anything begins.

What is the difference between a facial and a chemical peel?+

A facial deep-cleanses, exfoliates, steams, massages and hydrates your skin, finished with a hydration mask — the Classic Facial is $85 for 55 minutes. A chemical peel is a professional Circadia peel solution that lifts away dull surface layers so fresher, brighter skin emerges; peels are $125–$475 for 55 minutes, including the consultation that matches the peel to your skin, with the exact price confirmed up front.
