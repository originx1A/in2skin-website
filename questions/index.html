Questions

How much does a facial treatment cost in Bolton?+

At In2Skin, the Classic Facial is $85 for 55 minutes and the Anti-Aging Facial is $125 for 80 minutes. Add-ons run $35 to $65, chemical peels run $125 to $475, and microneedling is $65.

What does a facial treatment do?+

A facial treatment deep-cleanses, exfoliates, steams, massages and hydrates your skin, finished with a mask matched to your skin that day. Skin leaves brighter, fresher and more hydrated — with a glow that lasts for days.

How long does a facial treatment take?+

The Classic Facial takes 55 minutes and the Anti-Aging Facial takes 80 minutes. Add-ons take 20 minutes each, and can be added to a facial treatment or booked on their own.

How often should I get a facial treatment?+

Every 4 to 6 weeks. Most clients make it a monthly ritual.

What is a first visit like?+

A consultation first, then the treatment — with every step explained as it happens. It is tailored to your skin that day.

What are Private Hours?+

Premier appointments reserved just for you — early mornings and evenings, by request. You pay the regular price of the services you pick, plus $15 per visit. Sundays the studio is closed.

Can I buy a gift certificate?+

Yes — gift a Classic Facial ($85), an Anti-Aging Facial ($125), The Works ($160), or any custom amount from $10. A certificate code is emailed to the recipient, ready to redeem when they book, and gift codes never expire. You can also buy a treatment voucher: a named facial treatment, paid ahead.

How do I book?+

Book online anytime on this website, or call (289) 212-4124. Cash or Interac e-transfer are also welcome at the studio. Advance booking is recommended to get the time you want.

Where is the studio?+

In2Skin is a private, one-on-one aesthetics studio in Bolton, Ontario. Appointments are by booking, and directions are shared when you book. Clients visit from Caledon, Kleinburg, Vaughan, Brampton, Nobleton, King City, Orangeville, Georgetown, Mississauga and across Toronto and the GTA.

What if I need to change my booking?+

Call (289) 212-4124 and the studio will move it for you.

What are the benefits of a facial treatment?+

A facial treatment deep-cleanses, exfoliates and hydrates your skin, boosts circulation and leaves skin brighter, softer and glowing. Regular facial treatments keep pores clear, help breakouts settle and slow the look of ageing — and the hour itself is deeply relaxing.

What should I expect during a facial treatment?+

A consultation first, then a deep cleanse, steam, extractions if needed, gentle exfoliation, a relaxing face, neck and décolletage massage, a hydration mask matched to your skin, and serum and moisturizer to finish. Every step is explained as it happens, and you can ask for anything to be gentler or skipped.

Why are your facial treatments less expensive than other Bolton spas?+

In2Skin is a small private studio — one room, one guest at a time — so a Classic Facial is $85 for 55 minutes.

Is red light therapy safe?+

Yes — red light therapy is non-invasive and painless, with no recovery time. You lie back under warm red light for 20 restful minutes. It supports collagen and a calmer, more even complexion.

What is reflexology?+

Reflexology is gentle, rhythmic pressure on points on your feet that map to the rest of your body — the arch to your back, the toes to your head and neck. It helps the whole body unwind, and many clients sleep beautifully that night. It is $35 for 20 minutes, on its own or added to a facial treatment.

How much downtime is there after a chemical peel?+

It depends on the strength of the peel. Gentle peels have little to no downtime — skin looks fresh right away. Deeper peels can mean a few days of flaking or peeling as fresh skin emerges. Your consultation matches the peel to your skin and confirms exactly what to expect, before anything begins.

What is the best facial treatment in Bolton?+

There is no single best facial treatment — the best first facial treatment at In2Skin is the Classic Facial, $85 for 55 minutes, tailored to your skin that day. If fine lines are your main concern, the Anti-Aging Facial, $125 for 80 minutes, adds firming massage and targeted serums.

Where can I get microneedling near Bolton?+

At In2Skin, a private, one-on-one aesthetics studio in Bolton, Ontario — call (289) 212-4124 to book. Microneedling is collagen induction therapy that prompts the skin’s natural repair and new collagen, improving the look of fine lines, acne scarring, uneven texture and enlarged pores over a series of treatments. It's $65 for 20 minutes, and Sarah picks the needle size (micro or nano) that's best for your face at the visit.

What is the difference between a facial treatment and a chemical peel?+

A facial treatment deep-cleanses, exfoliates, steams, massages and hydrates your skin, finished with a hydration mask — the Classic Facial is $85 for 55 minutes. A chemical peel is a professional Circadia peel solution that lifts away dull surface layers so fresher, brighter skin emerges; peels are $125–$475 for 55 minutes, including the consultation that matches the peel to your skin, with the exact price confirmed up front.
