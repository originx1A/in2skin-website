Reflexology

Home · Treatments · Reflexology

Points on your feet map to your back, neck & shoulders. Gentle pressure helps your whole body unwind.

What’s included

  • Warm, comfortable setup and a moment to settle in
  • Gentle, skilled pressure across the reflex points of the feet
  • Attention to the points connected to your areas of tension
  • A calm, unhurried finish so the relaxation lasts

Benefits

  • Deep relaxation that spreads through the whole body
  • A lighter, calmer, more balanced feeling
  • The perfect finishing touch to any facial

Questions

How does it feel?+

Gentle, rhythmic pressure — calm spreads through your whole body in minutes.

What can my feet tell me?+

Each foot area maps to the body — arch to back, toes to head & neck. Tender points show where you hold tension.

How will I feel afterwards?+

Calm, light & rebalanced. Many clients sleep beautifully that night.

Can I add it to my facial?+

Yes — 20 minutes, the loveliest finish to any facial.

What is reflexology?+

Gentle pressure on points on your feet that map to the rest of your body — arch to back, toes to head & neck — helping your whole body unwind.
