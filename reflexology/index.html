Questions

How does it feel?+

Gentle, rhythmic pressure — calm spreads through your whole body in minutes.

What can my feet tell me?+

Each foot area maps to the body — arch to back, toes to head & neck. Tender points show where you hold tension.

How will I feel afterwards?+

Calm, light & rebalanced. Many clients sleep beautifully that night.

Can I add it to my facial?+

Yes — 20 minutes, the loveliest finish to any facial.

What is reflexology?+

Gentle pressure on points on your feet that map to the rest of your body — arch to back, toes to head & neck — helping your whole body unwind.
